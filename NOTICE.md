# Notice

D2ES-Net and its three proposed mechanisms are described in the accompanying
manuscript:

> D2ES-Net: Scene-Adaptive ANN-SNN Routing and Scattering-Evidence-Guided
> Spiking for SAR Ship Detection

This private snapshot contains only the project-specific method implementation,
documentation, and representative exports. The full experimental integration
and trained checkpoints are intentionally withheld until the manuscript is
accepted.

The research prototype was developed with standard open-source PyTorch
detection utilities, which are distributed under AGPL-3.0. Their required
notices and corresponding-source obligations will be retained when the complete
project is publicly released. The presence of those utilities does not transfer
authorship of the D2ES-Net mechanisms described in the manuscript.
